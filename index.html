What We Do

Our Services

From pollution control to salvage and underwater operations — professional teams, specialized equipment, and rapid mobilization in compliance with international standards.

01

Marine Oil Pollution Response

Rapid response to marine oil spill emergencies.

Professional oil spill response for ports, harbors, offshore facilities, and vessels in distress. Our experienced response teams contain, recover, and mitigate hydrocarbon pollution while minimizing environmental impact.

Our Capabilities

  • Oil Spill Containment Booms
  • Oil Recovery Skimmers
  • High-Capacity Transfer Pumps
  • Absorbent Pads and Absorbent Booms
  • Temporary Storage and Recovery Solutions
  • Trained Oil Spill Response Specialists
  • Shoreline Protection and Cleanup Operations

Protecting the marine environment through fast, reliable, and professional oil spill response.

02

Vessel Collision Emergency Response

Immediate assistance following marine collisions.

Marine collisions can result in structural damage, flooding, pollution, and operational disruptions. We provide specialized emergency response services to stabilize affected vessels and support recovery operations.

Our Services Include

  • Emergency Damage Assessment
  • Oil Spill Response and Pollution Control
  • Heavy Lifting and Rigging Operations
  • Temporary Repairs and Structural Stabilization
  • Dewatering and Flood Control
  • Salvage Equipment Deployment
  • Experienced Marine Response Personnel

Delivering dependable support when every minute matters.

03

Commercial Diving & Underwater Services

Professional diving solutions above and below the waterline.

A complete range of commercial diving services performed by qualified and experienced divers using industry-approved equipment and procedures — in ports, harbors, rivers, and offshore environments.

Diving Services

  • Underwater Inspection and Surveys
  • Underwater Welding
  • Underwater Cutting
  • Hull Repairs and Patching
  • Propeller and Rudder Inspection
  • Underwater Maintenance
  • Salvage Support Operations
  • Search and Recovery Services

Trusted underwater expertise for critical marine operations.

04

Marine Fire Fighting & Emergency Support

Expert intervention when fire threatens lives, vessels, and the environment.

Specialized marine firefighting support through DG Shipping-approved personnel trained in shipboard fire emergencies, working alongside vessel crews, port authorities, and emergency responders.

Capabilities

  • Vessel Fire Assessment
  • Fire Suppression Support
  • Boundary Cooling Operations
  • Emergency Response Coordination
  • Salvage Fire Support
  • Post-Fire Damage Assessment
  • Safety and Risk Management

Professional marine firefighting support when safety is critical.

05

Specialized Pumping Operations

High-capacity marine pumping solutions.

Specialized pumping services for flooding control, cargo transfer, salvage support, and emergency dewatering operations — designed for reliability in demanding marine environments.

Equipment Highlights

  • Specialized Hydraulic Pump Systems
  • Capacity up to 180 Cubic Meters per Hour
  • Heavy-Duty Discharge Hoses
  • Portable and Rapid Deployment Units
  • Flooded Compartment Dewatering
  • Cargo and Liquid Transfer Operations

Powerful pumping solutions for complex marine emergencies.

Grounded Vessel Response & Refloating

Grounded vessel assessment and refloating services.

Grounding incidents require careful planning, technical expertise, and specialized equipment. We provide comprehensive support from initial assessment through successful refloating operations.

Our Expertise Includes

  • Grounding Assessment and Survey
  • Stability and Structural Evaluation
  • Tank Blowdown Operations
  • Hydrocarbon Extraction and Transfer
  • Cargo Removal and Lightening
  • Refloating Planning and Execution
  • Salvage and Recovery Support

Delivering practical and effective solutions for stranded vessels worldwide.
